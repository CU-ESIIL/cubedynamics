**Historical intake index, October 12, 2026.** Companion source/protocol/context
URLs now route to consolidated canonical pages; use [the reviewer guide](../joss_review.md).

The short JOSS draft carries the software contribution. The longer manuscript
and SI carry design history, contracts, source distinctions, protocol details,
and dated evidence. This map gives every numbered section, table, and figure
a documentation home while retaining the complete originals and references.

**September 25, 2026 evidence boundary**.

The six linked companion pages below adapt the longer material into readable
website documentation. Existing generated references retain ownership of live
signatures and catalog facts. Historical results are attributed to their
recorded revisions, not relabeled as fresh validation.

## Editorial items remain editorial

The supplied JOSS draft includes pre-submission instructions about release
wording, archival gaps, funding, and AI disclosure. They are preserved for
collaborators to review, rather than treated as instructions to publish,
modify release metadata, change scientific assumptions, or remove exports.
The manuscript's author responsibility and funding statements need human
confirmation. The PDF disclosure and JOSS disclosure differ in their account
of completed human review; retaining both does not resolve that discrepancy.

This is a content migration with complete source preservation, not a new
scientific evaluation or certification. Exact signatures remain generated;
historical evidence remains dated; outstanding archival work remains visible.
