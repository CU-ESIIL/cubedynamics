# Unresolved scientific decisions

These are discussion decisions, not implementation defects.

1. What one pixel in the final product should mean, and whether one pixel needs multiple named values.
2. Which second-stage overlap operator is scientifically interpretable and robust across time and space.
3. How local, regional, continental, and global representations should coexist.
4. Whether hot/cold definitions remain pixel-relative, become region-relative, or are represented at multiple scales.
5. How to retain absolute climate context alongside relative-extreme synchrony.
6. How much local variance a continental/global representation must preserve by design.
7. How observation-window sensitivity should be evaluated without claiming one magic radius.
8. What temporal replication, station holdout, spatial-block holdout, and independent-product evidence are sufficient.
9. Why warm station-vs-PRISM pair agreement is weak in the current preliminary branch.
10. When an experimental aligned-view layer is mature enough for a public CubeDynamics verb.

The current endpoint is deliberately: *we understand the object well enough to decide how it should scale.*
